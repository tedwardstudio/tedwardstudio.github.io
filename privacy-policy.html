Privacy Policy

Cascade Blocks · Tedward Studio · Effective date: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Cascade Blocks (“the App”), made by Tedward Studio (“we”, “us”), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The App does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location, and we do not run our own servers that receive your data.

2. Information stored only on your device

Your best score, last score, unfinished game, and settings (music, sound, vibration, tutorial progress) are saved locally on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the App removes them.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: full-screen ads after a game ends and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch to continue a game. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version);
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data (for example, which ads were shown or tapped, crashes related to ads).

This information is collected and used by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: the App asks for permission before the IDFA can be used for personalized ads. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized using the IDFA. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: a consent form from Google is shown where required by law, letting you accept or refuse personalized advertising.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

6. Data security and retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete the App. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google’s policies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
